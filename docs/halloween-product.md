# Product

## Platform
Web: existing Next.js SRM Team Robocon website.

## Seasonal experience
Temporary Halloween homepage for website visitors. Live camera and local photo uploads produce a downloadable two- or three-photo strip with matching frames. Photos remain in the browser. SRM Robocon logo is the corner watermark. One switch removes the seasonal experience. Other routes retain their existing behavior.

## Confirmed direction
User selected a haunted robotics laboratory, atmospheric motion without jump scares, and a physical booth with automatic three-shot capture followed by strip decoration.
