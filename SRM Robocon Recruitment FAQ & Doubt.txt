SRM Robocon Recruitment FAQ & Doubt Dataset
Recruitment Overview

The SRM Robocon recruitment test is conducted offline at UB 5th Floor from 5:15 PM to 6:45 PM. Students can apply for up to two domains and attempt exams accordingly. The test is written-based and focuses on domain fundamentals, aptitude, logic, and robotics-related concepts.

Common Questions & Answers
1. Can I attend the test on either day?

Yes. If you are attempting only one domain, you may attend on either of the two recruitment days.

2. How many domains can I attempt?

You can attempt a maximum of two domains only.

3. Can I attempt one domain on Day 1 and another on Day 2?

Yes, provided you have not exceeded the two-domain limit.

4. What is the exam timing?

The exam runs from 5:15 PM to 6:45 PM.

5. What happens if I arrive late?

Late entry is allowed, but no extra time will be given after 6:45 PM.

6. Where is the exam conducted?

UB 5th Floor.

7. Is the test online or offline?

The recruitment test is completely offline.

8. Is the exam written or interview-based?

The first round is written.

9. Is ID card compulsory?

Yes. College ID card is mandatory. In some cases, an ID photo may be accepted.

10. Is there any fixed syllabus?

No fixed syllabus is provided. Students are advised to prepare fundamentals of their selected domain.

Frequently Asked Doubts (Additional)
“Can I write only one of my selected domains?”

Yes.

“Can I attempt both domains on the same day?”

Yes, if time permits.

“Will there be MCQs?”

Yes, many domains include MCQs.

“Will there be subjective questions?”

Yes, especially in coding and technical domains.

“Do I need to bring rough sheets?”

Usually no.

“Can I use calculator?”

Generally no unless specifically instructed.

“What should first years prepare?”

Basics, aptitude, and logical thinking.

“What should second years prepare?”

Core technical concepts and practical implementation.

“Will exact topics be revealed?”

No.

“Is there a passing mark?”

Selection depends on overall performance and competition level.

“How many students are selected?”

No fixed intake.

“Can I attend only on Day 2?”

Yes, but valid reasons may be expected if you skipped Day 1.

“Can I write the test from home?”

No. It is offline only.

“What if I forgot my ID card?”

Photo of ID may sometimes be accepted.

“Do coding domains always require laptop?”

No. Only specific software/web-related domains require laptops.

“What if I have classes during exam time?”

You may attend the other recruitment day if necessary.

“Will sample questions be shared?”

No.

“How should I prepare?”

Revise fundamentals, aptitude, and robotics-related basics for your domain.
