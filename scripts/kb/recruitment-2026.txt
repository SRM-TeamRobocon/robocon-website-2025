SRM Team Robocon — Recruitment 2026 Knowledge Base

Key Dates (Tentative)
Orientation: August 30, 2026.
Recruitment written exam: August 31 and September 1, 2026 (two days).
Venue: University Building (UB), 5th Floor.
Timing: 5:15 PM onwards (last year's exam ran until 6:45 PM — expect a similar window this year).
These dates, the venue and the timing are all tentative and may change — always check the latest announcement on Instagram (@srmteamrobocon) or official college mail closer to the date.

How many domains can I apply for?
You can select a maximum of 2 domains at registration. If you're attempting both, you can attempt both on the same day if time permits, or split them across the two exam days — one on August 31 and one on September 1.

What are the domains?
SRM Team Robocon recruits into 6 domains across 4 divisions:
- SPACED (Systems Programming Analysis and Computation Engineering Division): Coding, Web Dev
- SIESED (System Integration and Electronics Subsystem Engineering Division): SIESED
- SAMBED (Structural Architecture and Mechanical Body Engineering Division): SAMBED
- MCSOCD (Media Creation and Social Outreach Creatives Division): Corporate, VFX/GFX

Is the recruitment test written or an interview?
The first round for every domain is a written exam, conducted offline on paper. There is no fixed syllabus — prepare the fundamentals of your chosen domain(s) plus general aptitude and logical reasoning. Exact topics and sample questions are not shared in advance.

Do I need to bring a laptop?
A laptop is required ONLY for the Web Dev and VFX/GFX domains. No other domain — Coding, SIESED, Corporate, or SAMBED — requires you to bring a laptop.

What is the written exam out of?
The exam is not out of 100 marks. The exact maximum marks will be announced closer to the exam date.

What should I bring to the exam?
Your college ID card and reqruitment qr is mandatory. In some cases a clear photo of your ID may be accepted if you've forgotten the physical card. Reach early — late entry may be allowed, but no extra time is given past the end of the exam window.

What happens after the written exam?
Evaluators enter marks per domain, and a cutoff-based shortlist is computed for each domain separately — clearing one of your two domains does not depend on the other. Shortlisted candidates are called for a walk-in interview. Selected candidates then go through a training period before being formally inducted as members.

How do I register on the website?
1. Go to the recruitment registration page and click "Continue with Google" — use your PERSONAL Gmail account here, not your SRM email.
2. Enter your SRM email (@srmist.edu.in) on the next step. You do not need to verify it with an OTP right away — you can continue straight to filling your profile.
3. Fill in your name, registration number, year, department, course, phone number, hostel or day-scholar details, LinkedIn URL, a password, and pick 1–2 domains.
4. Submit — your account is created immediately and you're taken straight to your recruit dashboard.
5. From the dashboard, you can verify your SRM email with an OTP whenever convenient. It's optional at signup but recommended to complete soon after.

Why use a personal Gmail and not my SRM email for Google sign-in?
Google sign-in is only used to pull in your name and give you a way to sign back in — your SRM email is collected separately in the very next step of registration. Signing in with an SRM Google account there can cause issues, so use a personal Gmail account for that button specifically.

About SRM Team Robocon
SRM Team Robocon is SRM's competitive robotics team, known for representing the university at DD Robocon (India's national ABU Robocon qualifier) and ABU Robocon. The team runs on four divisions:
- SAMBED handles mechanical design, fabrication and structural engineering of the robots.
- SPACED handles embedded systems programming (Arduino, STM32, ESP32, Raspberry Pi, Jetson), robotics algorithms such as localisation, path planning and PID control, and the team's own Web Development.
- SIESED bridges SAMBED and SPACED — electronics, PCB design, circuit and signal integration.
- MCSOCD (covering the Corporate and VFX/GFX recruitment domains) runs content, design, video, sponsorship, PR and event management for the team.

Why join SRM Team Robocon?
You get hands-on experience building real competition robots from scratch, not just coursework. Recent highlights include an All-India rank of 13 among 110+ teams at DD Robocon 2025 at IIT Delhi, a "Best Hardware Prize" at the Automation and Robotics Challenge 2026 for an adaptive prosthetic gripper, strong showings at Smart India Hackathon and other hackathons, and flagship in-house workshops such as SolidWorks and Altium run by the team itself. New recruits go through structured cross-domain training before induction, so no prior robotics experience is required — just interest and a willingness to learn.

Where can I ask more questions?
Use the "Ask a Doubt" chat on the recruitment page or your dashboard, or follow the official Instagram @srmteamrobocon for the latest announcements.
